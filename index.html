Auto Subtitles & Caption Maker

Kapto is an iPhone app for adding automatic captions to vertical video. It recognizes the speech in your clip on the device, so the video is never uploaded, times every word, and burns animated captions in 16 styles into a copy you save to Photos, or exports the timed text as an .srt file. There are no credits or per-minute packs: you can import a clip, correct the words and preview every style for free, and a Pro subscription is only needed to save the result.

What Kapto does

  • Speech recognition on your iPhoneThe transcript is made on the device. Your video is never uploaded, so there is no queue and no waiting on a server. Which spoken languages work depends on your iOS version and the speech models installed.
  • Word-by-word timingEvery word gets its own time, so the highlight lands when the word is spoken, not when the line appears.
  • 16 caption stylesPlates, outlines, glows, marker and wipe highlights, and word-by-word arrivals. All of them can be previewed on your own clip before you pay.
  • Fix the words with chipsThe transcript is a row of word chips. Tap a word to correct a name or jargon before the captions are burned in.
  • Export to Photos or .srtSave the captioned video to your photo library in 9:16, 4:5 or 1:1, or export the timed subtitles as a SubRip .srt file. Both need Pro.
  • No credits, no per-minute packsOne subscription, billed monthly or yearly. You never buy minutes.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best app for automatic captions on an iPhone?

It depends on what you need. If you only want accurate, good-looking captions on a vertical video without a full editor, Kapto is built for that: on-device transcription, word-by-word highlight, 16 styles and .srt export. If you need a complete editor with effects and templates, apps such as CapCut or InShot cover more ground.

Is my video uploaded anywhere?

No. Speech recognition and caption rendering both run on your iPhone, so the video stays on the device.

Does Kapto charge per minute or use credits?

No. There are no credits and no per-minute packs. Pro is a subscription: $4.99 per month with a 3-day free trial, or $29.99 per year with a 7-day free trial.

What is free and what needs Pro?

Importing a clip, on-device transcription, editing the words and previewing all 16 styles are free. Saving the captioned video to Photos and exporting the .srt file need Pro.

Can I export an .srt file?

Yes. Kapto exports the timed text as a SubRip .srt file from the Export screen. This is a Pro feature.

Does it translate or dub my video?

No. Kapto transcribes the language spoken in the clip and burns those captions in. It does not translate or dub.

Which languages can it recognize?

That depends on your iOS version and the speech recognition languages available on your device, so we do not quote a fixed number. Check Settings on your iPhone for the languages iOS offers.

Is there an Android or web version?

No. Kapto is an iPhone app only.
