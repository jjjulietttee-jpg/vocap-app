A Caption App for iPhone Without Credits or Per-Minute Limits

Updated October 2026 · By the Kapto developer

Short answer. Kapto does not use credits or per-minute packs. You can import a video, transcribe it and preview all 16 styles for free; saving the captioned video or the .srt needs Pro, which is $4.99 per month (3-day free trial) or $29.99 per year (7-day free trial).

Step by step

  1. Try it freeImport a clip and let Kapto transcribe it on your iPhone. No credits are spent.
  2. Preview every stylePlay the highlight in any of the 16 styles on your own video.
  3. Start Pro when you are ready to saveChoose monthly ($4.99, 3-day free trial) or yearly ($29.99, 7-day free trial) on the paywall.
  4. Save as many videos as you likePro is a flat subscription, not a minute balance. Cancel any time in your App Store subscriptions.

Frequently asked questions

Is there any per-minute charge?

No. Pro is a subscription and is not metered by minutes.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. The monthly plan has a 3-day free trial and the yearly plan has a 7-day free trial, for new subscribers who are eligible.

How do I cancel?

In your iPhone's App Store subscription settings, at least 24 hours before renewal.

What stays free?

Import, on-device transcription, word editing and previewing every style.

Is Kapto available on Android?

No. Kapto is an iPhone app only.

Does Kapto translate or dub captions?

No. It captions the speech in the language spoken in the clip; it does not translate or dub.

Who makes Kapto and how do I get help?

Kapto is published by Anastassiya Shugaley. Contact details are on the support page.
