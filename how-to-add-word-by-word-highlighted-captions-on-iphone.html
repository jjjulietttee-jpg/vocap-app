How to Add Word-by-Word Highlighted Captions to a Video on iPhone

Updated November 2026 · By the Kapto developer

Short answer. Use a caption app that times every word, such as Titlecard (listed on the App Store as Kapto). Import the video, let it transcribe on your iPhone, pick a style with a word highlight (colour, pop, marker or wipe) and save the captioned video to Photos. Previewing the highlight on your own clip is free; saving needs Pro.

Step by step

  1. Import your videoPick a clip from your photo library, or try the built-in sample first. The video is not uploaded.
  2. Let it time every wordTitlecard recognizes the speech on the device and gives each word its own start and end time, so the highlight lands when the word is spoken.
  3. Correct the wordsThe transcript is a row of word chips. Tap a chip to fix a name or a term the recognizer got wrong.
  4. Choose a highlight styleAmong the 16 styles, the highlight can switch the spoken word to a new colour, pop it, fill a marker block behind it, or wipe the colour across it. Some styles also bring words in one by one.
  5. Pick the frame and saveChoose 9:16, 4:5 or 1:1, then save the captioned video to Photos (Pro) and post it to Reels, TikTok or Shorts.

Frequently asked questions

Can captions highlight each word as it is spoken?

Yes. Titlecard times every word and offers highlight styles that change the colour of the spoken word, pop it, fill a marker block behind it or wipe colour across it.

Do I have to pay to see the word highlight?

No. All 16 styles, including the highlight, preview free on your own clip. Saving the captioned video and exporting .srt need Pro.

Is the highlight burned into the video?

Yes. The saved video has the captions and the highlight rendered into it, so it plays the same on any platform. The .srt export is plain timed text and does not carry the highlight style.

Is my video uploaded?

No. Transcription and rendering run on your iPhone.

Can I fix a wrongly recognized word?

Yes. Tap the word chip and edit it before saving.

Which frame sizes can I save?

9:16, 4:5 and 1:1.

Is Titlecard the same as Kapto?

Yes. The App Store listing may still show the name Kapto while the rename to Titlecard rolls out.

Does it work on Android?

No. Titlecard is an iPhone app only.
