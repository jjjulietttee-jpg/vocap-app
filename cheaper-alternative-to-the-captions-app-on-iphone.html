Cheaper Alternative to the Captions App for iPhone Auto Subtitles

Updated November 2026 · By the Kapto developer

Short answer. If you only need automatic subtitles, Titlecard (listed on the App Store as Kapto) is cheaper than the Captions app: Pro is $5.99/week, $4.99/month or $49.99/year, while Captions lists $5.99 weekly, $9.99 monthly and $64.99 yearly on the US App Store (checked 2026-11-09). The trade-off is scope: Titlecard only adds captions, while Captions is a full AI video editor with avatars and dubbing.

Step by step

  1. List what you use Captions forIf it is only subtitles on clips you already shot, you are paying for an editor you do not use. If you rely on AI actors, avatars, dubbing or B-roll, keep Captions.
  2. Compare the plansTitlecard Pro: $5.99/week (3-day free trial), $4.99/month or $49.99/year. Captions: Basic $5.99/week, $9.99/month, $64.99/year; Max $24.99. Check the App Store for your country.
  3. Try Titlecard free on your own clipImport a video, let it transcribe on the iPhone and preview all 16 styles without paying.
  4. Export when you are happySubscribe to Pro only to save the captioned video to Photos or export an .srt file. There are no credits or per-minute packs.

Frequently asked questions

What is a cheaper alternative to the Captions app?

For subtitles only, Titlecard: $4.99/month or $49.99/year versus Captions at $9.99/month or $64.99/year (US App Store, checked 2026-11-09).

What do I lose by switching from Captions to Titlecard?

Everything that is not captioning: AI actors and avatars, AI edit with B-roll, dubbing and the broader editor. Titlecard adds captions only.

Does Titlecard charge per minute or in credits?

No. One subscription, billed monthly or yearly; you never buy minutes.

Is Titlecard free to try?

Yes. Import, on-device transcription, editing words and previewing all 16 styles are free; saving the video and .srt need Pro.

Is my video uploaded?

No. Speech recognition and rendering run on your iPhone.

Does Titlecard support as many languages as Captions?

Not necessarily. Captions lists captions in 91+ languages; Titlecard supports the spoken languages your iOS version offers on the device, and it does not translate.

Is Titlecard the same as Kapto?

Yes. The App Store listing may still show the name Kapto while the rename rolls out.

Is there a free caption app with no watermark?

We do not make claims about other apps' watermarks. Check the listing of each app; Titlecard previews for free and saves with Pro.
