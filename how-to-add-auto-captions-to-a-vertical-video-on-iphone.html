How to Add Auto Captions to a Vertical Video on iPhone

Updated October 2026 · By the Kapto developer

Short answer. Open Kapto, import your vertical video and let it transcribe the speech on your iPhone. Fix any wrong words, choose one of 16 caption styles and save the captioned video to Photos. Importing, transcribing and previewing are free; saving needs Pro.

Step by step

  1. Import your videoPick a clip from your photo library, or try the built-in sample clip first.
  2. Wait for the transcriptKapto recognizes the speech on the device and times every word. The video is not uploaded.
  3. Correct the wordsTap any word chip to fix a name or a term the recognizer got wrong.
  4. Pick a styleChoose from 16 styles and watch the highlight play on your own clip. Choose 9:16, 4:5 or 1:1 framing.
  5. SaveExport the captioned video to Photos (Pro), then post it.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to trim the video first?

No. Import the clip as it is; Kapto transcribes whatever speech is in it.

Can I change the words before saving?

Yes. The transcript is a row of word chips, and you can edit any of them.

Can I see a style before paying?

Yes. All 16 styles preview on your own clip for free. Saving the result needs Pro.

Which frame sizes are supported?

9:16, 4:5 and 1:1.

Is Kapto available on Android?

No. Kapto is an iPhone app only.

Does Kapto translate or dub captions?

No. It captions the speech in the language spoken in the clip; it does not translate or dub.
